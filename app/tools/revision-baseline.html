Display

Explore one connection at a time.

  1. Begin with User. Select the starting card to reveal its outgoing connections.
  2. Follow a highlighted outline. Steady outlines show direct connections; pulsing outlines show the possible step after that. Icon colors identify subsystems.
  3. Read the right panel. It explains the role, required inputs, outputs, authority and failures. Select a next connection there to continue.
  4. Add layers when needed. The top layer buttons reveal Memory, Learning, Tools, Recovery and Specialists.
